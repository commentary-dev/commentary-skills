# Source notes

Last reviewed: 2026-08-23

- Pew Research Center, *Writing Survey Questions*: wording, open versus closed questions, response options, order effects, stable measures, and pretesting. https://www.pewresearch.org/writing-survey-questions/
- W3C WAI Forms Tutorials: explicit labels, available instructions, accessible validation, and logical multi-page progress. https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/
- GOV.UK Design System, *Making labels and legends headings*: focus pages on one piece of information, decision, or question. https://design-system.service.gov.uk/get-started/labels-legends-headings/
- GOV.UK Design System, *Checkboxes*: conditionally reveal only simple related questions and move complex follow-ups to another page. https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/checkboxes/
- UK Government Analysis Function, *Questionnaire design guidance*: logical ordering, filtering, cognitive testing, usability testing, and testing the whole flow in its delivery mode. https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/questionnaire-design-guidance/
