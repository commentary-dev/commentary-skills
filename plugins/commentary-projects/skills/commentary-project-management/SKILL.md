# Commentary Project Management

Discover the credential-granted workspace first. Projects are optional: do not create one when direct document review meets the request. Identify the Project, visibility, lifecycle, current ETag and requested outcome. Team visibility defaults to workspace; participants visibility limits discovery and requires the existing human governance path for membership changes.

Create objectives and context; link accessible original Resources rather than copying them. A Resource may belong to several Projects. Before linking, independently read the destination Resource and original provider authority. Read related Projects before proposing a relation; a relation is organizational context, not delegation or an access grant.

Use discovered list/get/create/update/resources/relations/status operations. Archive/restore and participant administration may be advertised but denied to Agents: route them to an eligible human rather than retrying with another identity. Report execution, artifact existence, review, exact acceptance and independent verification separately, including outstanding Decisions and unavailable source content.

Read [references/operating-contracts.md](references/operating-contracts.md) before transport selection or recovery. Use [references/worked-example.md](references/worked-example.md) for the validated pm-tracker workflow and its limits.
