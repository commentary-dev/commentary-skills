# Product research method selection

| Question | Evidence | Suitable methods |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What are users trying to accomplish and why? | Qualitative, attitudinal and behavioral context | Interviews, contextual inquiry, field observation |
| Can realistic users complete a task? | Qualitative behavioral evidence | Moderated or unmoderated usability testing |
| Does a concept communicate the intended value? | Limited-use qualitative evidence | Concept test followed by neutral probing |
| How often does a behavior or attitude occur? | Quantitative evidence | Instrumented analytics, survey, benchmarking, or experiment with adequate sample size |
| How does an experience unfold over time? | Natural-use longitudinal evidence | Diary-style study or repeated contextual sessions |
| Why did a measured outcome change? | Mixed evidence | Analytics or benchmark plus interviews or usability observation |

Commentary can compose interviews, usability studies, concept tests, surveys, diary studies, card sorting, tree testing, and heuristic evaluation from source-backed Content, Activity, Form, and Review surfaces. Analytics, A/B tests, eye tracking, and specialist instrumentation remain external evidence integrations; do not claim Commentary collects them natively.
