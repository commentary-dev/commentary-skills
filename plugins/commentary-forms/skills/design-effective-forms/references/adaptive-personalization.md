# Adaptive per-respondent Forms

Use an adaptive follow-up only when it increases relevance, reduces burden, or safely clarifies an answer.

- Keep the purpose, consent, and stable core measures fixed across respondents.
- Generate only the minimum follow-up needed at an authored transition boundary.
- Do not infer sensitive traits or ask about topics outside the stated purpose.
- Do not use a prior answer to steer the respondent toward a preferred conclusion.
- Keep question tone, option balance, accessibility, and validation quality consistent with authored sections.
- Explain adaptation when a respondent would reasonably be surprised by it.
- Record the generated section, source answers, agent provenance, and contract version for audit and interpretation.
- Use a deterministic authored fallback when generation is unavailable, invalid, or unsafe.

Comparative or longitudinal studies should not personalize the wording or order of core measures. Limit adaptation to clearly separated follow-up evidence.
