# Commentary Inbox Triage

Use this skill to inspect outstanding attention, summarize history, and continue work at its canonical Resource. Install `commentary-inbox-triage` directly or use the `commentary-inbox` plugin.

Canonical instructions: [SKILL.md](../skills/commentary-inbox-triage/SKILL.md).

## Workflow

Discover the available CLI/HTTP surface and credential-granted workspace. Read bounded Active or History pages with supported filters, sorting, and unchanged opaque cursors. Group attention by Resource and explain the next useful step without copying private content unnecessarily.

Open the canonical Review, Form, Research item, or Interaction through its dedicated surface. An Inbox item is an attention pointer: dismissal, snooze, read state, and assignment do not establish a Decision or source permissions.

Current-task replies, exact requested Decisions, and future agent guidance have different purposes. Resume the existing correlated Interaction rather than creating duplicate requests.

## Optional attention proposals

Saved views and attention policies require explicit requested configuration work. Use advertised typed proposals and simulation; proposals remain disabled until a human approves them. Simulation and notification history are observations, not permission to send messages or publish policy.

Keep capability checks explicit. Browser account visibility can span workspaces, while agent discovery is restricted to the credential grant. See [operating surfaces](../skills/commentary-inbox-triage/references/operating-surfaces.md) and [attention proposals](../skills/commentary-inbox-triage/references/attention-proposals.md).
